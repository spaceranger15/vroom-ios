# Validation and known limits

## Evidence available

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone screenshots supplied by the developer | The shown interfaces were present in the installed prototype | A reproducible build, uninterrupted background tracking or error-free operation |
| Core test runner: 13/13 passed at the reviewed checkpoint | Deterministic cases for pay aggregation, summaries, fuel intervals, hourly rates, mileage filtering, offer/source rules and mission/feedback behavior | Full SwiftData/UI integration, camera accuracy, battery life or notification reliability |
| Separate OCR Lab device runs and saved-text replays | Diagnosis of recognition versus spatial-assignment errors on those images | General real-world pump/offer accuracy or superiority on other devices |
| Local backup/restore implementation | A merge-based path exists in the source | Recovery under every migration, interruption or malformed-file case |

The source review and core test run occurred on 2026-10-23. No iOS Simulator,
release archive, independent security audit or App Store submission was performed
for this showcase. Screenshots were not generated by a simulator or AI.

## Why outcomes are not an accuracy score

Drivers choose which offers to take and which outcomes to verify. Passed offers
have unknown counterfactual outcomes. A favorable rating can miss a target, and
an unfavorable rating can still be accepted. The history preserves this feedback
without claiming that the system learned an optimal policy or caused an earnings
increase. Local outcome counters are not published in this gallery.

## Before a wider release

1. Compare mileage and interruptions with known routes across locked-screen,
   pause/resume, relaunch and low-battery scenarios.
2. Exercise home/station notifications, permission-denied states and every action.
3. Verify Shortcuts/Live Activities for expired, repeated, missing and queued offers.
4. Round-trip complete backups into clean and existing installations.
5. Evaluate consented real pump photos; preserve manual verification and unknown
   fields rather than manufacturing a number.
6. Profile repeated scans for memory and battery behavior, then test accessibility
   and supported device sizes.

Public presentation of this project is separate from an app release. The
prototype is not described as App Store-ready or commercially validated.
